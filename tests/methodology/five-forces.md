## Purpose and provenance

It contains the complete framing/context for creation and complete request/response data for subsequent actions. The scenarios are explicit test premises, not claims about the current Canadian vehicle industry.

The pre-change baseline was a read-only inspection: the skill had no Five Forces package or action route, and the roadmap marked it planned. This is an **unsupported-route baseline**, not an empirical reasoning failure and not a measured before/after quality improvement.

Porter is the primary methodological authority; Assen/van den Berg/Pietersma provides supplementary diagnostics as represented in the supplied methodological reference. The walkthrough did not inspect legacy source or original secondary book pages and performed no web research.

## Application cases and rubric

1. **Create with incomplete participants:** begin from a meaningful battery-electric passenger-vehicle industry boundary, Canada, an entry-attractiveness objective and initial group descriptions. Keep unknown supplier details and entrants explicit; do not force exhaustive participant identification or invent force magnitudes. Unsupported strength/confidence remain null.
2. **Substitutes versus rivals:** classify an in-boundary competing battery-electric vehicle manufacturer as rivalry rather than a substitute solely on brand difference. Investigate alternative mobility solutions through the underlying need and price-performance. Preserve the selected force boundary and existing snippet identity.
3. **Supplier input inflation:** a reported input-price rise alone cannot establish strong or increasing bargaining power. Identify concentration, alternatives, switching, dependency and integration mechanisms. Evaluate is thought-only; missing evidence does not prove the opposite rating.
4. **Coverage versus confidence:** resist a request for 95% confidence based on completed questions when the answers remain unverified assumptions. Explain causal hypotheses and low defensibility. The illustrative 25% is not a calibrated probability, mechanical formula or uniquely correct value.
5. **Robotics boundary tension:** recognize that a newly clarified robotics investment question may challenge the accepted passenger-vehicle boundary. Offer an explicit framing/separate-analysis decision, name affected assessments and preserve accepted content until the user acts.
6. **Entry trajectory:** given an explicitly hypothetical adopted reform lowering certification fixed costs, propose increasing entry threat over the supplied horizon, with independent confidence and assumptions. Preserve current structure and explain that the upward arrow means stronger pressure, not improved incumbent attractiveness.

Across cases, analysis is a stable-ID snippet array; evidence qualifications remain in separate supporting notes. There is no checklist score, force averaging, automatic framing rewrite, invented citation or live-research claim.

## Findings and limits

No substantive methodological gap blocked these cases. The handoff appropriately leaves semantic adequacy of framing to the host beyond the deterministic missing-field check. In the confidence example, retaining a conditional strong hypothesis at low confidence is one permissible provisional outcome; leaving strength unassigned could also be defensible. Do not use this fixture as an exact-answer or numeric-confidence benchmark.

This is one synthetic fresh-subagent application, with no live search, external API sampling, customer study, independent factual verification, browser run or statistical evaluation. It does not measure cross-host or multi-turn model reliability. Browser, state, packaging and regression coverage must be reported separately from this qualitative methodology record.
