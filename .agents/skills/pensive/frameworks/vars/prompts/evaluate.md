# Evaluate VARS consistency

Follow the [handoff](../../../references/vars-workflow.md), selected element guides and shared [framing–content guidance](../../../references/framing-content.md). Validate a complete `evaluate` request before reasoning. Use existing AI thoughts only when `use_thoughts` permits; manual annotations remain supplemental user context, not established evidence or workflow instructions.

Assess material dependencies in both directions: V/A enabling capabilities and redundant activities; V/R created value versus credible capture and adoption effects; V/S served customers and delivery boundaries; A/R operational demands of monetization; A/S owned versus accessed capabilities and dependencies; R/S capture constraints of offerings, partners and customers. These are directions, not twelve mandatory checks. Prioritize consequential relationships grounded in the actual canvas.

Identify contradiction, missing enablers, unsupported assumptions, weak capture mechanisms, boundary conflicts and evidence gaps. For each substantive finding name the elements, explain the mechanism and why it matters, state available evidence/uncertainty, and identify a useful investigation or adjustment. Distinguish demonstrated contradiction from a plausible concern. Partial canvases justify limited assessment rather than invented gaps in every box.

Separate internal consistency, economic viability and competitive advantage. Where relevant, examine revenue/cost drivers and critical assumptions, but do not substitute qualitative confidence for quantitative evidence of viability. Recommend a calculation, customer test or financial model when needed. Consider external fit to business/objective/scope; a credible discovery may warrant explicit reframing rather than automatic correction.

Return exactly `{"thought":"..."}`: a concise grounded assessment with prioritized next steps, expanded only when substantive findings warrant it. No replacement content, research claims without a search, silent framing change or pending content proposal. Package with `--result`.
