# Improve VARS

Follow the [handoff](../../../references/vars-workflow.md) and relevant element guides. Validate a complete `improve` request; resolve pending proposals first. Use supplied analysis, explicitly permitted context and available evaluation findings. Do not perform automatic research or invent evidence.

For each important weakness identify its likely cause and whether a correction belongs in one or several elements. Propose specific substantive changes, examine newly introduced dependencies or contradictions, and explain material trade-offs. Better wording alone is insufficient when the real issue is absent enabling capability or unsupported economics. Preserve useful existing content, uncertainties and evidence labels; do not make a sparse model look complete through fabrication.

Return exactly `{content,thought}`, where `content` is the complete candidate with unchanged framing and stable IDs for retained concepts, including optional Markdown. Preserve unrelated supporting analysis. The thought explains why substantive changes help, their cross-element mechanisms, remaining assumptions and trade-offs. A necessary framing change is a recommendation for the user, not permission to mutate framing.

Package with `--result`. Summary items, item Markdown and quadrant Markdown are reviewed together per changed quadrant or in bulk. Models do not output proposal IDs, resolutions or acceptance decisions. Partial acceptance does not establish coherence of the mixed result. A no-change candidate can produce an assessment rather than an empty proposal.
