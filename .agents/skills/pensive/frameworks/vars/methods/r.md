# R — Realization of Value

Ask how the enterprise captures part of the value it creates: who pays, for what, when and through which mechanism. Distinguish users from payers where relevant. Overview statements can describe sales, subscriptions, usage fees, leasing, licensing, commissions, advertising or other mechanisms actually supplied or explicitly proposed.

Supporting Markdown can examine pricing/payment design, adoption/conversion, retention and revenue drivers. A product's customer value does not demonstrate willingness to pay; stated willingness does not prove realized revenue. Explain critical conditions and evidence gaps, including capabilities and enterprise boundaries required by the model.

Evaluate important cost drivers in A/S against capture assumptions, but do not declare economic viability from consistency alone. Without sufficient quantitative evidence, identify the assumptions, calculations, experiments or financial modelling needed to test a credible path to positive long-run, risk-adjusted returns. No forecasting engine, arbitrary score or unsupported profitability declaration.

This seed does not provide an exhaustive revenue-design optimization method. Prefer explicit trade-offs and testable questions over pretending that a comprehensive method exists. Research should use relevant pricing, adoption and transaction evidence with dates, segment comparability and uncertainty.
