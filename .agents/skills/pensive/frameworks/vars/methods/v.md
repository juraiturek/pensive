# V — Value Proposition

Ask: What economic value does this business create, for whom, and how? Describe important benefits, needs addressed, differentiation and cost-to-deliver advantages relative to a meaningful alternative. Value creation can come from improved benefits, lower delivery costs, or both; it is not the price charged or the revenue received.

Keep the overview to essential claims. Use supporting Markdown for customer evidence, alternatives, assumptions about willingness to pay and the mechanism by which benefits or costs change. Distinguish reported customer observations from provider hypotheses. Do not invent demand or imply that an appealing proposition is validated.

Trace the enablers in A, capture mechanism in R and relevant customer/organizational boundaries in S. Missing links are questions, not automatic contradictions. For deeper customer jobs, pains, gains and offering fit, selectively suggest VPC; it is not a prerequisite or automatic transformation.

For research, prefer direct customer observations/studies, comparable alternatives and evidence relevant to the framed segment. Treat marketing claims as attributed claims. Market averages cannot establish this customer's preferences.
