# S — Scope of Enterprise

Ask where this business model draws its boundaries. Consider customer scope (served and deliberately excluded segments), horizontal scope (included/excluded offerings) and vertical scope (internal activities versus outsourced/partner access). Discuss geography only where material to the framed model.

Keep essential choices in the overview. Supporting Markdown can examine make-versus-buy, quality/control, partner dependencies, economies of scale/scope and trade-offs. A partner's capability is not automatically an owned enterprise capability; recognize access and contractual/operational uncertainty.

Trace whether boundaries enable V, fit A, and support R. A proposed scope change may alter costs, capabilities and capture opportunities; explain the mechanism rather than assuming integration or outsourcing is better. Empty or unresolved choices are valid.
