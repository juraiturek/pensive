# Research Five Forces

Use the shared [Research method](../../../prompts/research.md), [handoff](../../../references/research-workflow.md), [Five Forces workflow](../../../references/five-forces-workflow.md), methodology and selected force guides. Validate full state, framing and explicit canvas/section target first. Search is executed by the host, never the page; unavailable search means disclose the limitation, never fabricate sources.

Use the same structural-driver questions as user guidance, analysis and evaluation. Search for industry-specific evidence, geography, participant groups, economics, counterevidence and relevant temporal context. Separate industry observations, firm-specific facts and unsupported inference. Rising input prices alone do not prove supplier power; rivals are not automatically substitutes. Assess evidence reliability and causal relevance rather than counting hits. Cite sources near factual claims and retain publication/as-of context where consequential.

Return exactly `{"report":"Markdown findings","sources":[{"title":"...","url":"https://..."}],"content": <optional complete candidate>}`. Report-only is valid. Candidate framing is immutable; only selected force(s) may change. Supporting research evidence/citations belong in research_notes and material causal implications in analysis. Takeaways excludes AI thoughts: a report-only finding does not silently become an accepted force conclusion. Do not turn recommendations into accepted strategy. Report and candidate require one atomic decision, guarded against stale content and opted-in contexts.
