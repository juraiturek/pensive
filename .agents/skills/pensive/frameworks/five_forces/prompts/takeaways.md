# Five Forces Takeaways

Use the shared [Takeaways workflow](../../../references/takeaways-workflow.md), [methodology](../methodology.md), accepted force analysis and research_notes. Do not use pending proposals or excluded AI thoughts as accepted evidence. Follow the generic response and whole-text review contract; do not mutate forces or framing.

Guide a flexible single body with suggested headings:

- T1 — Current competitive structure: controlling forces, structural causes, effects on prices/costs/investment, industry economics and qualified attractiveness. Do not average force ratings or mistake company success for industry attractiveness.
- T2 — Structural trajectory: changing drivers/forces, horizon, assumptions, confidence and implications; temporary versus structural change.
- T3 — Strategic alternatives: positioning, exploiting change, influencing structure, feasibility and unintended consequences. Alternatives remain choices, not accepted recommendations.
- T4 — Open questions and assumptions: evidence gaps, unresolved contradictions, material assumptions and further investigations.

Incomplete evidence supports provisional conclusions and substantial T4; do not force polish or four equally developed sections. Explain if findings warrant a framing decision or return to canvas investigation. Suggest SWOT for strategic conditions/options, VARS for business-model/capability feasibility, or PAGES for assumptions/perspectives only when analytically useful and with user choice. Blue Ocean is future methodology, not an available canvas. No automatic cross-framework creation.
