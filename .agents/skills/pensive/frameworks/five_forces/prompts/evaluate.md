# Evaluate Five Forces consistency

Read the [workflow](../../../references/five-forces-workflow.md), [methodology](../methodology.md), relevant force guides and shared framing fit. Validate complete state and minimally sufficient framing before analysis. Full canvas is context; an explicit force target focuses findings.

Internally examine observation → mechanism → implication → strength, confidence defensibility, cross-force contradictions, unsupported assumptions and controlling forces receiving too little attention. Externally examine industry/product and geographic boundary, participant segmentation and available industry economic evidence, including long-run profitability where relevant. Distinguish firm advantage from industry attractiveness. An apparent conflict requires investigation, not automatic correction. Missing evidence constrains conclusions; it does not establish the opposite claim.

For trajectories assess driver change, horizon, assumptions, cross-force effects and temporary versus structural developments. Uncertain is not stable. Flag Takeaways claims unsupported by accepted analysis. Surface reframing proposals with reasons and affected conclusions, preserving the user’s framing and judgments. Prioritize issues and useful next investigations. No fabricated search or mandatory balanced checklist.
