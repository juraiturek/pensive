# PAGES Takeaways

Read the framing and relationships among Parts, Actions, Goals, Events and Self-concepts. Identify a consequential reinterpretation, tension or direction the current configuration supports, and explain how a change in one dimension would alter the meaning or implications of another. If there is no supported before-and-after comparison, describe the current perspective or a conditional shift. Connect the resulting implication to the original situation and purpose. Treat a plausible perspective shift as a different interpretation with consequences and limits.
