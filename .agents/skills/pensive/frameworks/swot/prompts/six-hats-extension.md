# Six Hats on SWOT/TOWS

Use the focal entity, objective and environment. Distinguish internal capabilities and weaknesses from external conditions. On an item target, use related factors or strategies to illuminate the selected item.

## White

Identify the missing comparison, source or boundary condition that determines whether the factor matters to this entity and objective.

## Red

Surface how the factor or strategy might land emotionally for a stakeholder grounded in the framing. Mark imagined reactions as impressions.

## Black

Stress-test the assumption that makes a strength usable or an opportunity attainable. Look for threat exposure hidden in a TOWS pairing.

## Yellow

Seek overlooked opportunities, a capability concealed by a weakness, or an opening created by a threat. Explain the conditions that make the upside plausible.

## Green

Cross-pair factors in an overlooked way or vary a constraint to expose a materially different strategic option.

## Blue

Identify the decision the matrix or pairing needs to inform. Isolate the comparison or question to address first.
