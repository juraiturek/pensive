# Research SWOT

Follow the [shared Research method](../../../prompts/research.md) and [Research handoff](../../../references/research-workflow.md). Resolve the selected factor, factor section or whole canvas against the submitted focal entity, objective and environment. Clarify consequential industry/geographic/time ambiguity before searching. Research is not TOWS generation; preserve strategy collections and framing.

For **opportunities** and **threats**, investigate external conditions relevant to the objective: customer/market changes, technology, regulation, economic conditions, competitors and ecosystem shifts. Explain why a condition helps or harms this entity; a general trend is not automatically an opportunity or threat. Prefer primary statistics, official policy/regulatory material, filings, original studies and direct evidence for the claim, with current event/publication dates where material.

For **strengths** and **weaknesses**, external evidence may reveal relevant comparators or publicly documented characteristics, but a competitor's strength is not the user's organization's strength or weakness. Ask the user to confirm whether the characteristic applies, and how their organization differs, before proposing it as a focal organizational S/W factor. A supplied explicit confirmation can justify a scoped candidate; without confirmation use a report-only result with applicability questions. Public company claims remain attributed claims, not independent capability verification. Do not label absence of public information a weakness.

Return `{report,sources,content?}`. A candidate contains complete SWOT content with only the selected target changed; retain identities and distinguish supported external conditions from interpretations/hypotheses. Whole-canvas research may propose supported factor changes, but not strategy or framing revisions. Approved findings are recorded as a research thought; this slice does not add Markdown fields to SWOT factors. Both report-only and content-changing results require one atomic approval batch. Broader strategy implications belong in the report or a subsequent Strategize/Improve request.
