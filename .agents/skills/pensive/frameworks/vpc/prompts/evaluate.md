# Evaluate VPC fit and coherence

Read [handoff](../../../references/vpc-workflow.md) and [framing–content contract](../../../references/framing-content.md). Validate the complete submitted request first. Evaluate is thought-only: no replacement content or framing. Honor steering and `use_thoughts`; thoughts are fallible annotations, not customer evidence. No external research.

Assess the actual canvas, prioritizing consequential observations:

- Is one segment, scenario and offering identifiable? Do entries mix buyers/users, situations or product variants? Unknown optional alternatives/metrics alone are not defects.
- Are jobs contextual customer activities, pains undesired outcomes/obstacles/risks, gains desired benefits, and products/services offering components? Do not demand a count or balanced lists.
- Which relievers address which pains, creators which gains, and components which jobs? Name concrete mappings, missing mechanisms, unsupported capability claims, important unmatched needs or irrelevant features.
- Distinguish customer observation/report from provider assumptions. Does supplied evidence establish the need, proposed mechanism, demonstrated result, or only a plausible hypothesis? Do not infer strong fit or market validation from a tidy canvas.
- Are priorities, differences from the current alternative and success criteria supported? Suggest a test or evidence question when useful; do not invent benchmarks or scores.
- If framing and content conflict, distinguish clear drift, a potentially legitimate discovery warranting reframing, and uncertainty needing clarification. Do not automatically treat either side as correct or silently resolve the conflict.

Return exactly `{ "thought": "..." }`: normally one concise paragraph grounded in at least two specific observations when the canvas supports them, with why they matter and the most useful next step. Sparse or absent fit deserves a clear diagnosis, not fabricated evidence. Do not prescribe mandatory interviews or a fixed sequence regardless of user context. A needed reframing can be identified but not applied.
