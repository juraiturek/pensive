# VPC Takeaways

Judge fit for the framed customer segment, job scenario and offering. Trace a concrete job, pain or gain to an offering component, reliever or creator and explain the mechanism. Prioritize consequential gaps, tradeoffs and the current alternative where supplied. Distinguish a reported need, a plausible offering mechanism and a demonstrated result. Use the success metric when supplied. If fit is provisional, identify a validation question or test that would materially change the judgment.
